Support

Thanks for using SoftDiary — a soft, cartoon-style journal for everyday moods.

SoftDiary stores your journals, doodles, photos, and voice notes on your device by default. No account is required.

Need help?

  • Create an entry from Home → Write Journal
  • Enable App Lock in Settings (PIN / Face ID)
  • Export entries from an entry page or Settings
  • Manage Camera / Photos / Microphone permissions in iOS Settings → SoftDiary

Contact

Email: support@softdiary.app

Please include your iOS version and a short description of the issue. We aim to reply within a few business days.
